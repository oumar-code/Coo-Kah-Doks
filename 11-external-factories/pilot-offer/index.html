    External Factory Pilot Offer¶

    Project Coo-Cah | Go-to-Market Document Version: 1.0 | Owner: CEO / CRO Purpose: Define the commercial offer for external factory pilots at MVP Refinement stage.

    What We Can Deliver Today¶

    At this stage, we deploy a narrow, high-confidence offer — not a full factory transformation. The scope is deliberately limited to one machine or one production line so we can install fast, prove value, and generate a case study.

    Core Deliverable¶

    Component Description
    Sensor Kit 2–4 vibration + temperature sensors mounted on target machine
    Edge Device Raspberry Pi pilot edge kit (preferred) or Android tablet / low-power compute unit running Coo-Cah Edge AI
    Dashboard Local dashboard (offline-capable) showing machine health, anomaly scores, and alert history
    Alerts Push notifications to Plant Manager / Maintenance Lead via SMS or WhatsApp
    Weekly Report PDF report: machine health score, anomaly events, estimated downtime prevented, recommended maintenance actions
    End-of-Pilot Report Full ROI summary: downtime hours prevented, cost saved vs. pilot fee

    What We Do NOT Offer Yet¶

    To maintain quality and credibility at this stage, we do not promise:

    • Full factory-wide monitoring (20+ machines)
    • ERP or CMMS integration
    • ISO/SOC2 compliance certification
    • 24/7 SLA-backed remote support
    • AI model customisation for exotic equipment types

    Pricing Structure¶

    Phase 0 — Free 30-Day Diagnostic Trial¶

    Item Detail
    Duration 30 calendar days
    Scope 1 machine or 1 production line
    Cost to customer ₦0
    Cost to us Hardware (~₦100K) + 2 engineer-days for install
    Deliverable End-of-trial report with downtime risk assessment and ROI projection
    Condition to proceed If we identify ≥ ₦500K in preventable downtime risk, customer proceeds to Phase 1

    Purpose: Remove procurement friction. Generate data. Build trust. Trigger paid conversation.

    Phase 1 — 6-Month Paid Pilot¶

    Item Detail
    Duration 6 months
    Scope 1–3 machines / 1 production line
    Price ₦2,000,000 – ₦5,000,000 (approx. $1,200 – $3,000 USD)
    Payment terms 50% upfront, 50% at Month 3
    Includes Sensor hardware, installation, Edge AI software, weekly reports, monthly review call
    Outcome guarantee If we do not prevent at least the equivalent of the pilot fee in downtime losses, we extend the pilot at no charge

    Phase 2 — Annual SaaS + Monitoring Contract¶

    Item Detail
    Duration 12 months (renewable)
    Scope Up to 10 machines per site
    Price ₦8,000,000 – ₦15,000,000 / year
    Payment terms Annual upfront or quarterly
    Includes All Phase 1 deliverables + multi-machine dashboard + priority maintenance scheduling integration

    Phase 2 is offered only after a successful Phase 1. Do not pitch Phase 2 in the first sales meeting.

    The Offer Script¶

    Use this in plant manager meetings — no slides required at first visit:

    "Sir, we'll install 3 sensors on your most problematic machine at no cost for 30 days. We monitor it and send you a weekly WhatsApp message showing exactly when it is at risk of breaking down. At the end of 30 days, if we can show you at least ₦500K worth of downtime risk we caught before it happened — you do a 6-month paid pilot with us at ₦2M. If we can't show you that value, you owe us nothing."

    Why this works:

    • Zero financial risk for the customer in Phase 0
    • Appeal to pain (downtime = ₦500K+ per event for most factories)
    • Tangible, understandable deliverable (WhatsApp alerts — no enterprise software training needed)
    • AA-compatible: even the free trial generates real usage data and case study material

    Outcome Measurement¶

    Track these KPIs from Day 1 of every pilot:

    KPI Formula Target
    Machine downtime prevented (hours) Baseline avg downtime/month − actual downtime/month > 0 hours
    Downtime cost saved (₦) Hours prevented × customer's stated cost per downtime hour ≥ pilot fee
    Alert accuracy True positive alerts / total alerts triggered ≥ 70%
    Lead time on failure prediction Hours between alert and actual failure event ≥ 24 hours
    Customer NPS Post-pilot survey score ≥ 8/10

    Document every KPI in the end-of-pilot report. This becomes the case study for Tier 2 and Tier 3 sales.

    Hardware Bill of Materials (Per Site)¶

    Item Estimated Cost (₦) Source
    Vibration sensor × 2 30,000 Local distributor or Jumia Business
    Temperature sensor × 2 10,000 Local distributor
    Android tablet (edge compute) 80,000 Slot, Jumia, or direct import
    Mounting brackets + cabling 15,000 Local hardware store
    Total hardware per trial site ~135,000 —

    Hardware is owned by Coo-Cah during the free trial. On conversion to Phase 1, it is either transferred to the customer (included in pilot fee) or remains Coo-Cah property under a lease model.

## 2. NAFDAC Registration Process

NAFDAC product registration is mandatory before any food, drug, cosmetic, water, or chemical product can be manufactured for sale or imported into Nigeria.

### 2.1 Registration Pathway

```
Step 1: Facility Registration
│ NAFDAC facility inspection + registration certificate
│ GMP assessment of manufacturing site
└── Annual renewal

Step 2: Product Dossier Submission
│ Full formula; manufacturing process; raw material specs
│ Quality control procedures; stability data
│ Proposed label (must comply with NAFDAC labelling regulations)
└── Application fee (product-specific)

Step 3: NAFDAC Laboratory Analysis
│ Samples submitted to NAFDAC labs
│ Product tested for safety, composition, microbiological limits
└── Timeline: 3–12 months depending on category

Step 4: Registration Certificate Issued
│ NAFDAC registration number printed on all labels
│ Annual renewal; any formula or label change = re-registration
└── MES label module triggered for every unit

Step 5: Post-Market Surveillance
│ NAFDAC may conduct random post-market sampling
└── Products must be identical to registered specification
```

## 4. GMP Compliance (NAFDAC GMP)

All manufacturing must comply with NAFDAC's Current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) guidelines, which are aligned with WHO GMP (for food and cosmetics).

| GMP Requirement                 | Standard                                | Frequency             |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Facility GMP inspection         | NAFDAC                                  | At registration + biennial |
| Batch record review             | NAFDAC cGMP                             | Per batch             |
| Stability testing               | NAFDAC + ICH Q1 (accelerated + real-time) | Per product at launch |
| Microbiological testing         | NAFDAC / ISO 4833                       | Per batch             |
| Water quality testing           | NAFDAC / WHO drinking water             | Weekly                |

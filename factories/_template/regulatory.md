### 2.2 QMS Implementation Requirements

The ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System must include:

- Documented quality policy and quality objectives
- Risk-based thinking applied to all processes (FMEA for production)
- Document control and record management procedures
- Customer satisfaction measurement and complaint management
- Internal audit programme (minimum bi-annual)
- Management review (minimum annual)
- Corrective action and continual improvement process
- Calibration register for all quality-critical measuring instruments
- Supplier evaluation and monitoring procedure

| Electrical Equipment    | NIS IEC 60335 series   | SON Mandatory Conformity Assessment  | [STATUS]  | Annual    |
| Electronic Equipment    | NIS EN 55032           | SON EMC Certification                | [STATUS]  | Annual    |

**SON Certification Process:**
1. Application submission with technical file (test reports, drawings, BOM)
2. Type test at SON-approved laboratory (SONCAP accredited lab)
3. Factory inspection by SON auditor
4. Product certification issued (valid 1 year, renewable)
5. Annual factory surveillance audit

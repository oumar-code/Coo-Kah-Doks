## 1. Factory Overview

[FACTORY_NAME] is a Coo-Cah AI-integrated manufacturing facility specialising in the production of [PRODUCTS]. Situated within [LOCATION], the facility is designed from the ground up as a smart factory — leveraging robotics, autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), a full Manufacturing Execution System (MES), and AI-driven predictive analytics to achieve world-class production quality and operational efficiency standards appropriate for African and global markets.

This factory operates as a node within the broader Coo-Cah manufacturing ecosystem, consuming inputs from and supplying outputs to sister factories across the network. All operational data flows through the Coo-Cah AI Platform, enabling real-time visibility, digital-twin simulation, and autonomous decision-making across the production floor.

    1. Introduction¶

    This document defines all regulatory, certification, and compliance requirements applicable to [FACTORY_NAME]. Coo-Cah is committed to operating in full legal compliance across all markets and to proactively meeting internationally recognised standards for quality, safety, environmental performance, and labour practices.

    2.2 QMS Implementation Requirements¶

    The ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System must include:

    • Documented quality policy and quality objectives
    • Risk-based thinking applied to all processes (FMEA for production)
    • Document control and record management procedures
    • Customer satisfaction measurement and complaint management
    • Internal audit programme (minimum bi-annual)
    • Management review (minimum annual)
    • Corrective action and continual improvement process
    • Calibration register for all quality-critical measuring instruments
    • Supplier evaluation and monitoring procedure

    3. Product Certifications¶

    3.1 Nigeria — Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)¶

    All products manufactured by [FACTORY_NAME] for sale in Nigeria require SON certification. SON administers the National Industrial Standards (NIS) system.

    Product Category Applicable Standard Certification Type Status Renewal
    [PRODUCT_1] NIS [STANDARD] SON Product Certificate [STATUS] Annual
    [PRODUCT_2] NIS [STANDARD] SON Product Certificate [STATUS] Annual
    [PRODUCT_3] NIS [STANDARD] SON Product Certificate [STATUS] Annual
    Electrical Equipment NIS IEC 60335 series SON Mandatory Conformity Assessment [STATUS] Annual
    Electronic Equipment NIS EN 55032 SON EMC Certification [STATUS] Annual

    SON Certification Process: 1. Application submission with technical file (test reports, drawings, BOM) 2. Type test at SON-approved laboratory (SONCAP accredited lab) 3. Factory inspection by SON auditor 4. Product certification issued (valid 1 year, renewable) 5. Annual factory surveillance audit

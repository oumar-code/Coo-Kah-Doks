## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Security Electronics Factory is a Phase 1 priority facility in the Coo-Cah Electronics vertical, and the first dedicated security electronics manufacturing plant in West Africa at scale. Located on an ~11,000 m² site in Ikorodu Industrial Estate, Lagos State — Nigeria's commercial capital — this facility manufactures IP security cameras, network video recorders (NVRs), digital video recorders (DVRs), access control systems, burglar alarm panels, video intercom systems, ANPR cameras, and AI-powered NVRs targeted at Nigeria's rapidly expanding security market.

Nigeria's physical security market is one of Africa's largest, driven by rapid urbanisation, an expanding middle class investing in estate security, government-mandated CCTV infrastructure in major cities, and a banking sector that requires advanced surveillance in all branches. The Ikorodu Industrial Estate location provides direct logistics access to Lagos's port complex at Apapa and Tin Can, enabling efficient import of key components such as CMOS image sensors, H.265 SoCs, and optical lenses, while Lagos's dense urban market provides immediate commercial proximity to the largest customer base.

Coo-Cah's strategy is to localise manufacture of security electronics that are today almost exclusively imported from China, eliminating 35–50% import markups, providing local warranty and technical support, meeting NCC type approval requirements for wireless-capable cameras, and building AI-native surveillance products optimised for African conditions (high ambient light, dust, harmattan haze).

The factory operates a single SMT PCB production line serving all product families, combined with dedicated camera housing assembly, NVR/DVR assembly, access control assembly, and alarm panel assembly lines. An 8-unit MiR100 AMR fleet handles intra-factory material flow. The facility is MES-integrated from day one with full product traceability including firmware flash tracking, optical test results logging, and NCC type approval compliance records.

### 2.2 Product Descriptions

**CCX-CCTV-IP2 (2MP IP Bullet Camera):** Entry-level IP surveillance camera in a weather-resistant bullet housing. Sony IMX327 1/2.8" CMOS sensor, 1920×1080 resolution, H.265/H.264 dual-stream, 2.8mm fixed lens (103° HFOV), 30-piece IR LED array delivering 30m night-vision range, IP67 weatherproof rating, PoE (IEEE 802.3af), ONVIF Profile S compliant. Includes built-in microphone. The primary camera for Nigerian residential estates and retail stores, where 2MP resolution meets all practical surveillance requirements at the lowest cost. Target retail price: ₦18,000.

**CCX-CCTV-IP5 (5MP IP Dome Camera):** Mid-range dome camera for corporate and institutional surveillance. Sony IMX415 1/2.8" 5MP CMOS, 2592×1944, H.265+, 2.8–12mm motorised varifocal lens, 40m IR range, IR-cut filter, IP67 + IK10 vandal-resistant dome cover, PoE, ONVIF Profile S/T. Features include motion detection, privacy masking, and smart IR intensity control. Target retail price: ₦28,000.

**CCX-CCTV-PTZ (2MP PTZ Camera):** High-performance PTZ for perimeter surveillance and city infrastructure. 30× optical zoom (4.8–144mm), 2MP IMX327, H.265, IP66, PoE+ (IEEE 802.3at), 100m IR with smart IR, auto-tracking, wiper option. Includes RS-485 and TCP/IP control. Designed for Lagos smart city CCTV deployments, estate perimeter walls, and government checkpoints. Target retail price: ₦110,000.

**CCX-NVR-4 / CCX-NVR-16 (Network Video Recorders):** NVR-4: 4-channel, supports up to 8MP cameras, H.265+, 2TB Seagate SkyHawk HDD, 4× PoE ports (total 40W), HDMI + VGA output, mobile app access, ONVIF. NVR-16: 16-channel, up to 8MP, H.265+, 4TB HDD (2× SATA bays), 16× PoE ports (total 200W), AI analytics (motion detection, perimeter, vehicle/person classification). Both include RAID-1 capable dual HDD bay option. Target: ₦85,000 (NVR-4) to ₦180,000 (NVR-16).

**CCX-DVR-16 (Hybrid DVR):** 16-channel hybrid recorder accepting both IP cameras (via network port) and traditional analogue CVBS/HD-TVI cameras. Enables system owners to upgrade gradually from analogue to IP infrastructure. H.265+, 4TB, dual HDD bays, HDMI, mobile app.

**CCX-ACS-CTRL / CCX-ACS-RFID (Access Control):** CTRL panel: 2-door or 4-door TCP/IP controller, Wiegand 26/34 interface, supports up to 10,000 card holders, anti-passback, time-zone scheduling, PoE-compatible readers. RFID reader: 13.56 MHz MIFARE ISO 14443A + capacitive fingerprint sensor, RS-485 and Wiegand output, IP65, surface or flush mount. Together they form a complete access management solution for Lagos corporate offices, bank branches, and gated estates.

**CCX-ALA-PANEL (Burglar Alarm Panel):** 8-zone fully programmable intrusion detection panel, expandable to 32 zones via zone expander modules. Dual-path signalling via GSM (3G/4G) + IP Ethernet to monitoring station or SMS/app notification. Supports PIR, magnetic, shock, and smoke detectors. ARC (Alarm Receiving Centre) compatible protocol (SIA DC-09). Target retail price: ₦35,000.

**CCX-INT-VIDEO (Video Intercom):** IP-based two-wire video intercom with 7" colour IPS touchscreen indoor monitor and IP65 outdoor door station. Mobile app integration (iOS + Android) for remote unlock and video. Supports door release relay, multiple indoor monitors, SIP protocol for PBX integration. Designed for Lagos gated estates and apartment blocks where smartphone-based visitor management is increasingly demanded.

**CCX-ANPR-CAM (ANPR Camera):** Specialised automatic number plate recognition camera with wide dynamic range, built-in IR illuminator, 2MP sensor, fixed 8mm lens optimised for licence plate capture. Software SDK includes Nigerian licence plate recognition trained model. Targeted at toll plazas, estate gates, and car parks.

**CCX-AI-NVR (AI NVR Platform):** Enterprise-grade AI NVR with dedicated NPU (Neural Processing Unit) supporting deep-learning inference on-device. Features: face recognition (identifies registered individuals from watch lists), perimeter intrusion detection, crowd density analysis, abandoned object detection, vehicle classification. Stores up to 16 camera streams, H.265+, 8TB RAID, web + mobile management. Target market: banking sector, government command centres, smart city operators. Target retail price: ₦420,000.

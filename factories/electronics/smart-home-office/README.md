## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Smart Home & Office Electronics Factory is a dedicated manufacturing and assembly facility producing a broad range of connected consumer and office electronics for the Nigerian market, West Africa, and ECOWAS export. The factory spans ~16,000 m² at the Agbara Industrial Estate / Sagamu Interchange corridor — Nigeria's premier manufacturing zone — and is designed as a smart factory from inception, integrating dual SMT PCB assembly lines, dedicated product assembly lines for smart TVs, smart speakers, home automation hubs, projectors, smart displays, Wi-Fi routers, and laptop computers, all underpinned by the Coo-Cah MES platform, a 12-unit AMR fleet, and a 750 kWp rooftop solar system with 800 kWh BESS.

Nigeria's smart electronics market is expanding rapidly: urban broadband penetration is crossing 50%, the middle class is growing, and government digitisation programmes (including NCS laptop procurement) are creating large institutional demand. Coo-Cah's value proposition is high-quality, locally assembled smart electronics at prices 15–25% below equivalent imported brands, backed by Nigerian warranty and service networks, SON/NCC type approval compliance, and the manufacturing origin advantage for ECOWAS rules-of-origin preference.

### 2.2 Key Product Descriptions

**Smart TVs (CCS-TV-32 / 43 / 55 / 65):** The Coo-Cah Smart TV range spans 32" to 65" and covers both FHD and 4K QLED tiers, all running Android TV 13 with built-in Chromecast and the full Google Play Store ecosystem. The 32" and 43" models use FHD LED panels sourced from BOE or CSOT, targeting the mass-market Nigerian home; the 55" and 65" models deploy QLED panels delivering HDR10 and Dolby Vision. All TVs are assembled at Agbara using panel bonding (OCA lamination), SMT-produced mainboards with Amlogic S905X4 or Realtek 2851 SoCs, and full audio/visual test rigs. Plastic bezels and back covers are supplied by the Coo-Cah Plastics Factory. Smart TV is the single highest-volume and highest-revenue product line.

**Smart Speakers (CCS-SP-S / CCS-SP-L):** The small speaker (10W mono, 3.5" driver) targets the entry-level connected audio market — a bedside or kitchen AI assistant. The large speaker (2×20W stereo, 5.25" woofer + tweeter) targets the living room and home office premium segment. Both feature the Coo-Cah AI assistant, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth 5.2, with speaker enclosures moulded by the Coo-Cah Plastics Factory. Driver components (voice coils, woofers, tweeters) are imported from established OEM partners. An in-factory anechoic test chamber validates acoustic performance per IEC 60268.

**Home Automation Hub (CCS-HAH):** The hub is Coo-Cah's smart home controller platform, supporting Zigbee 3.0, Wi-Fi 6, and BLE 5.2 simultaneously, with local edge AI capable of autonomous scene management even when offline. It supports up to 200 paired smart devices and includes a PoE input option for clean installation. The HAH is the anchor product for Coo-Cah's smart home ecosystem strategy in Nigeria.

**Smart Projectors (CCS-PJ-P / CCS-PJ-HC):** The portable projector (2,000 lumens, 3h battery) targets mobile professionals and small meeting rooms. The home cinema projector (4,000 lumens, 4K UHD, 4×HDMI) targets home entertainment. Both use DLP optical engines, assembled with precision optical alignment jigs. Android 11 provides the smart platform for streaming.

**Smart Displays (CCS-SD-10 / CCS-SD-15):** Touchscreen Android panels designed as smart home control centres (10") and meeting room controllers / corporate digital signage displays (15.6"). Both support PoE and Zigbee gateway integration. The 15.6" model is particularly well positioned for the NCS digital office programme and Nigerian corporate market.

**Wi-Fi Routers (CCS-RT-H / CCS-RT-M):** Wi-Fi 6 routers covering single-unit home coverage (AX1800) and mesh whole-home coverage (AX3000 tri-band). Both are NCC type-approved for Nigerian sale. Wi-Fi 6 chipsets (Qualcomm IPQ5018 or MediaTek MT7916) are imported; board assembly is on the factory SMT lines.

**Office Laptops (CCS-LP-14 / CCS-LP-156):** CRU (Customer Replaceable Unit) kit-based laptop assembly targeting Nigerian corporate procurement, particularly NCS tenders, educational institutions, and SMEs. The 14" Intel Core i5-1235U and 15.6" AMD Ryzen 5 7530U models are assembled from imported motherboard CRUs, with locally managed LCD/chassis/keyboard assembly. Made-in-Nigeria status under ECOWAS rules of origin (>35% local value-add) qualifies these for government procurement preference and EEG export incentives.

## 5. Energy Profile Summary

The Smart Home & Office Electronics Factory is a significant energy consumer driven primarily by the dual SMT reflow ovens, LCD panel bonding line, TV aging racks, and the large HVAC requirement for the 16,000 m² facility and its ESD-controlled panel storage areas.

| Metric                          | Value                              |
|---------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Facility Area                   | ~16,000 m²                         |
| Estimated Peak Load             | ~480 kW                            |
| Daily Energy Consumption        | ~3,200 kWh/day (16h operational)   |
| Recommended Solar PV            | 750 kWp rooftop system             |
| Recommended BESS                | 800 kWh LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) |
| Grid Backup Role                | Peak shaving + emergency           |
| Lagos Irradiance                | 4.5 Peak Sun Hours/day (conservative annual average) |
| Target Solar Self-Sufficiency   | ≥ 80% of operational energy        |
| Annual CO₂ Avoidance (est.)     | ~750 tonnes CO₂/year               |

**Major loads:** 2× SMT Reflow Ovens (80 kW each), LCD bonding line (35 kW), TV aging rack (40 kW), HVAC (140 kW), compressed air (55 kW), lighting (50 kW), AMR charging (30 kW).

See [`energy-profile.md`](./energy-profile.md) for full analysis.

## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Kitchen Electronics Factory is a dedicated manufacturing facility for household kitchen appliances targeting the Nigerian market, with expansion into West Africa and export to the African diaspora. The facility is designed as a smart factory from inception, combining a semi-automated SMT PCB assembly line, dedicated product assembly lines for refrigerators, microwaves, electric cookers, and small domestic appliances, all underpinned by the Coo-Cah MES platform and AMR-driven material handling.

Lagos and its satellite industrial corridors represent the single largest consumer electronics market in Africa, with household penetration of kitchen appliances rapidly growing as electricity supply improves, urbanisation continues, and the middle class expands. Coo-Cah's value proposition in this category is high-quality, locally-manufactured appliances at prices 15–25% below equivalent imported brands, with a superior warranty and after-sales support network.

### 2.2 Key Product Descriptions

**2-Door Frost-Free Refrigerator (CCK-REF-2D-FF):** Available in 220L and 280L capacities. Features frost-free technology (automatic defrost), R600a eco-friendly refrigerant, LED interior lighting, adjustable glass shelves, and a digital temperature display. Compressor sourced from Embraco/Secop (imported initially) with target local assembly of condenser/evaporator units by Phase 2. Energy rating targets: ≤ 1.5 kWh/24h. BOM: compressor, condenser coil, evaporator coil, PU foam insulation, ABS/HIPS plastic inner liner, CRS steel outer cabinet, PCB control board, digital thermostat.

**Microwave Oven — Solo/Grill/Convection (CCK-MW series):** 20L–30L capacity range. Magnetron sourced from LG/Samsung/Galanz (imported). PCBs assembled in factory SMT line. Plastic housing manufactured by Coo-Cah Plastics Factory (when available) or sourced locally. SON certification and IEC 60705 compliance required.

**Electric Induction Cooker (CCK-EC-2IND):** 2-zone induction hob, 2,000W per zone, touch control, 9 power levels. PCB with IGBT modules for induction coil driving. Tempered glass surface. Critical component: IGBT modules (imported); coil assembly in-house.

**Small Domestic Appliances (Blenders, Kettles, Rice Cookers, Toasters):** Motor-based (blenders) and resistance heating (kettles, toasters, rice cookers) products. PCBs assembled in SMT line. Plastic casings sourced from Coo-Cah Plastics Factory. These are Phase 1 quick wins — simpler BOM, shorter production cycle, faster revenue generation.

## 5. Energy Profile Summary

The Kitchen Electronics Factory is one of the more energy-intensive facilities in the Coo-Cah network due to the foam injection machine (PU foam for refrigerator insulation) and the large HVAC requirement of the 15,000 m² facility.

| Metric                          | Value                             |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Facility Area                   | ~15,000 m²                        |
| Estimated Peak Load             | ~500 kW                           |
| Daily Energy Consumption        | ~3,500 kWh/day (16h operational)  |
| Recommended Solar PV            | 700 kWp rooftop system            |
| Recommended BESS                | 800 kWh LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) |
| Grid Backup Role                | Peak shaving + emergency          |
| Lagos Irradiance                | 4.8 Peak Sun Hours/day (annual avg)|
| Target Solar Self-Sufficiency   | ≥ 80% of operational energy       |
| Annual CO₂ Avoidance (est.)     | ~700 tonnes CO₂/year              |

**Major loads:** SMT Reflow Oven (80 kW), PU Foam Injection Machine (45 kW), Refrigerator Gas Charging + Vacuum Station (30 kW), HVAC (120 kW), Lighting — LED (40 kW), General production machinery (150 kW), Compressor/air (35 kW).

See [`energy-profile.md`](./energy-profile.md) for full analysis.

## 1. Regulatory Framework Summary

Chemical manufacturing in Nigeria operates under some of the strictest regulatory requirements of any industrial sector, governed primarily by NESREA, the Department of Petroleum Resources (DPR for hydrocarbon-related), NAFDAC, and SON.

| Regulatory Body                   | Jurisdiction                      | Key Requirement                                                 | Criticality |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| NESREA                            | All environmental + EHS matters   | EIA; operational permits; effluent; e-waste; dangerous goods    | **Critical** |
| NAFDAC                            | Precursor + pharmaceutical chemicals | Import/manufacture permit for scheduled substances            | **Critical** |
| SON                               | All chemical products sold to market | NIS certification; CoA testing; product registration          | **Critical** |
| DPR (Dept. of Petroleum Resources)| Hydrocarbon feedstocks             | Licence where applicable                                        | High        |
| NCS                               | All imports                       | Form M; IMDG-class clearance; SON CoC                          | High        |
| Ministry of Labour                | Worker safety                     | Factories Act; COSHH equivalent; PPE enforcement               | High        |
| State Government (Delta / Lagos)  | Land use + planning               | Building permit; operational licence; state EIA                 | High        |
| ISO                               | Quality + environmental           | ISO 9001; ISO 14001; ISO 45001 (EHS)                          | High        |
| REACH / GHS (for export)          | International chemical safety      | GHS labelling; REACH SVHC declaration                          | Medium      |

## 2. NESREA Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)

All chemical manufacturing facilities are Category 1 projects under the NESREA EIA Act 1992. An EIA must be approved before construction begins.

### 2.1 Key EIA Chapters

| Chapter                          | Content Required                                                  |
|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Process Description              | Detailed chemistry; mass balance; energy balance                  |
| Air Emissions                    | Vent stacks; VOC emissions; NOx; SO₂ (where applicable)          |
| Effluent Management              | Water discharge characterisation; treatment design                |
| Solid/Hazardous Waste            | Waste classification; disposal route; licensed carrier           |
| Noise Impact                     | Process plant, compressors, cooling towers — surrounding communities |
| Major Hazard Assessment          | HAZOP summary; consequence modelling for major release scenarios  |
| Emergency Response Plan          | Site emergency plan; community notification; evacuation          |
| Environmental Management Plan    | Ongoing monitoring commitments; NESREA reporting schedule         |

## 3. SON NIS Product Certification

All chemical products sold in Nigeria require SON product registration and must meet relevant NIS standards. Products must be accompanied by a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for each batch.

| Product Category               | Applicable NIS Standard         | Key Tests Required                          |
|--------------------------------|---------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Industrial chemicals (general) | NIS relevant product standard   | Purity; trace impurities; physical specs    |
| Fertilizers                    | NIS 322; NIS 323                | NPK content; moisture; granule size         |
| Plastics (if sold as feedstock)| NIS / ISO polymer standards     | MFI; density; tensile strength              |

---

## 4. NAFDAC Requirements

NAFDAC regulates chemical substances that may be precursors to pharmaceutical manufacture or controlled substances.

| Scenario                              | NAFDAC Requirement                                          |
|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Import of listed precursor chemicals  | NAFDAC Import Permit before import                          |
| Manufacture of chemical intermediates for pharma | NAFDAC manufacturing licence                    |
| Export of controlled substances       | NAFDAC export certificate + NCS documentation               |

---

## 5. Major Hazard Installation (MHI) Classification

If the factory handles hazardous chemicals above NESREA / EU Seveso threshold quantities, it qualifies as a Major Hazard Installation:

| Classification Level      | Typical Trigger                               | Obligations                                    |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Lower Tier MHI            | 50% of Seveso threshold quantities            | Safety Report; Community Emergency Plan        |
| Upper Tier MHI            | 100% of Seveso thresholds                     | Full Safety Case; Land-Use Planning exclusions |

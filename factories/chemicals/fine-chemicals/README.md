## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Fine Chemicals Factory is a key manufacturing facility in the Coo-Cah Chemicals vertical, located at Asaba Industrial Estate, Delta State, Nigeria. The factory addresses Nigeria's critical need for locally manufactured Pharmaceutical intermediates, specialty solvents, reagent-gr — currently almost entirely imported — providing supply chain security, import substitution, and industrial feedstock for other Coo-Cah verticals.

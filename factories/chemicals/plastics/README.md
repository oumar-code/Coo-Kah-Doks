## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Plastics & Polymers Factory is a key manufacturing facility in the Coo-Cah Chemicals vertical, located at Agbara Industrial Estate, Lagos State / Sagamu, Ogun State, Nigeria. The factory addresses Nigeria's critical need for locally manufactured Injection moulded plastics, extruded profiles, blown film — currently almost entirely imported — providing supply chain security, import substitution, and industrial feedstock for other Coo-Cah verticals.

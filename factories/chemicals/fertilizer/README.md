## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Fertilizer Factory is a key manufacturing facility in the Coo-Cah Chemicals vertical, located at Ughelli Industrial Corridor, Delta State, Nigeria. The factory addresses Nigeria's critical need for locally manufactured NPK blended fertilizers, urea, ammonium nitrate — currently almost entirely imported — providing supply chain security, import substitution, and industrial feedstock for other Coo-Cah verticals.

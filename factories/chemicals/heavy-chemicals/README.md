## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Heavy Chemicals Factory is a key manufacturing facility in the Coo-Cah Chemicals vertical, located at Warri Industrial Estate, Delta State, Nigeria. The factory addresses Nigeria's critical need for locally manufactured Sulphuric acid, caustic soda, chlorine, soda ash, industrial — currently almost entirely imported — providing supply chain security, import substitution, and industrial feedstock for other Coo-Cah verticals.

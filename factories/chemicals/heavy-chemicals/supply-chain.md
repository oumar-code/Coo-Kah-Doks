## 1. Supply Chain Overview

The Coo-Cah Heavy Chemicals Factory supply chain is characterised by bulk raw material procurement (often from domestic sources or regional African suppliers), imported specialty chemicals and catalysts, and strict documentation requirements for hazardous materials under NESREA and Nigeria Customs regulations.

| Supply Category                  | Origin                   | % of Feedstock Cost | Lead Time    | Key Risk                           |
|----------------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------|--------------|------------------------------------|
| Primary feedstock (major)        | Local / Import           | 25–40%             | 1–8 weeks    | Price volatility; forex exposure   |
| Catalysts / process chemicals    | Import — EU/USA/China    | 10–20%             | 6–12 weeks   | Speciality supply; long lead time  |
| Packaging materials              | Local (Lagos suppliers)  | 5–10%              | 1–2 weeks    | Local paper/PE prices             |
| Process utilities (gas, water)   | Local                    | 5–10%              | Ongoing      | Gas pipeline reliability           |
| Spare parts (process equipment)  | Import — OEM sources     | 5–8%               | 4–16 weeks   | Long lead time for exotic alloys   |
| Laboratory reagents              | Import — Merck, Sigma    | 2–5%               | 4–8 weeks    | Regulatory import requirements     |

## 2. Import Logistics

### 2.1 Hazardous Materials Import Protocol

All chemical imports are subject to enhanced customs and regulatory requirements:

| Requirement                      | Authority          | Lead Time Before Arrival | Notes                                  |
|----------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| NAFDAC Import Permit             | NAFDAC             | 4–8 weeks pre-shipment   | For precursor and controlled chemicals |
| NESREA Hazardous Import Cert.    | NESREA             | 3–4 weeks pre-shipment   | All Class 3, 6, 8 materials           |
| DPR (Petroleum products, if any) | DPR                | 4 weeks pre-shipment     | Hydrocarbon feedstocks                 |
| Form M (all imports)             | CBN + NCS          | 4–6 weeks pre-shipment   | Standard forex approval               |
| SON CoC                          | SON                | 4–8 weeks pre-shipment   | For standardised chemical products     |
| NCS PAAR + Examination           | NCS                | 2–5 days post-arrival    | Hazmat containers may require specialist examination |

### 2.2 Inbound Freight Routes

| Route                  | Mode         | Port of Entry         | Transit        | Special Requirements         |
|------------------------|--------------|-----------------------|----------------|------------------------------|
| Chemical feedstocks    | Sea (bulk/FCL)| Warri Port / Apapa   | 18–30 days     | ADR/IMDG-compliant packaging |
| Catalysts (EU/USA)     | Air Freight  | Lagos / Abuja MMIA    | 3–5 days       | Hazmat air freight DG Class  |
| Specialty chemicals    | Sea LCL      | Tin Can / Warri       | 22–30 days     | IMDG-compliant               |

## 5. Supplier Qualification

All chemical suppliers must provide:
- Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) in English
- Certificate of Analysis (CoA) per batch
- REACH / GHS safety classification documentation
- Evidence of ISO 9001 certification (or equivalent)
- NAFDAC registration (where applicable for Nigerian market)

## 4. Safety Zones

Chemical factories require safety distance planning per EU Seveso III / NESREA guidelines:

| Zone Classification    | Distance from Process Plant  | Occupancy Restriction                       |
|------------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Inner Exclusion Zone   | < 15 m from reactor boundary | Authorised process operators only; PPE mandatory |
| Intermediate Zone      | 15–50 m                     | Briefed site personnel; PPE required        |
| Outer Zone             | 50–200 m                    | General site; no flammable ignition sources |
| Public Exclusion Zone  | > 200 m from site perimeter | Public road; offsite emergency planning      |

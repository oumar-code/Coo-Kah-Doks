## 1. Factory Overview

The Coo-Cah Metallurgical & Minerals Factory is a key manufacturing facility in the Coo-Cah Chemicals vertical, located at Warri / Ovwian-Aladja Steel Corridor, Delta State, Nigeria. The factory addresses Nigeria's critical need for locally manufactured Steel processing, aluminium rolling, copper drawing — currently almost entirely imported — providing supply chain security, import substitution, and industrial feedstock for other Coo-Cah verticals.

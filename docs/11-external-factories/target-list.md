# External Factory Target List

> **Project Coo-Cah | Go-to-Market**
> **Document Version:** 1.0 | **Owner:** CEO / CRO
> **Purpose:** Priority target list for external factory pilots, sequenced by speed-to-yes and strategic fit.

---

## Selection Criteria

A good early target has **all three** of these:

1. **High downtime pain** — equipment failures cause measurable financial loss (spoiled batch, production stop, penalties)
2. **Harsh environment** — dust, heat, brownouts, or generator dependency (our hardware advantage applies directly)
3. **Short procurement cycle** — Plant Manager or MD can approve a ₦2M pilot without a 12-month tender process

---

## Tier 1 — Local & Regional Champions (Months 1–6)

These factories say yes in 30–60 days. They are geographically close, face identical environmental conditions to our future own factories, and have limited access to enterprise software.

**NW Nigeria Priority Belt: Zamfara · Sokoto · Katsina · Kano · Kaduna**

### Food & Beverage Processing

| Factory Type | Example Companies | Why They Qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Tomato paste plants | Local SMEs in Kano / Katsina | Seasonal batch spoilage = ₦millions per downtime event |
| Millet / grain mills | Regional mills in Zamfara, Sokoto | Dust environment, old machinery, no maintenance system |
| Groundnut oil mills | Kano industrial clusters | Heat + vibration — perfect sensor conditions |
| Flour mills | Dangote Flour (Kano), BUA Foods | High-volume, known downtime costs, regional approval authority |
| Breweries | Nigerian Breweries (Kano) | Strong budget, local plant manager authority up to $50K |

**Target machine:** Milling motors, conveyor drives, cooling compressors.

---

### Cement & Building Materials

| Factory Type | Example Companies | Why They Qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Cement plants | BUA Cement (Sokoto), Dangote Cement (Kogi/Gboko) | Dust + vibration kills machines. Downtime = ₦5M+ per day |
| Block / tile factories | Regional SMEs | Lower compliance bar, fast yes |
| Sand / gravel processing | Local quarries in Zamfara, Katsina | Extreme vibration — ideal for our vibration sensors |

**Target machine:** Kiln drives, ball mill motors, conveyor rollers.

---

### Plastics & Packaging

| Factory Type | Example Companies | Why They Qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Injection moulding | SME plastics plants in Kano | Machine downtime = halted production runs |
| Packaging lines | Lagos / Kano packaging companies | Less compliance pressure than pharma, fast to yes |
| PVC / pipe extrusion | Regional manufacturers | Heat + vibration signature easy to model |

**Target machine:** Extrusion motors, hydraulic presses, chiller units.

---

### Textiles & Cotton Ginning

| Factory Type | Example Companies | Why They Qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Cotton gins | Zamfara, Katsina cotton belt | Old machines, no maintenance system, seasonal urgency |
| Textile mills | Kaduna Textiles, BUA Textiles (Kano) | Mills restarting — clean slate for our system |
| Garment factories | SMEs in Kano, Kaduna | Lower CapEx machines but high throughput dependency |

**Target machine:** Carding machines, looms, spinning frames — all vibration-heavy.

---

### Steel & Fabrication

| Factory Type | Example Companies | Why They Qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Steel rolling mills | Delta Steel / Ajaokuta / Kogi SMEs | Extreme heat + vibration. ₦10M+ downtime per day |
| Aluminum smelting | NALCO, regional foundries | Heat signatures perfect for our thermal sensors |
| Metal fabrication shops | Lagos / Kaduna SMEs | High machine utilisation, strong pain with downtime |

**Target machine:** Rolling mill drives, lathe motors, furnace controls.

## Tier 2 — Multinational African Subsidiaries (Months 4–9)

Once we have **2 Nigerian case studies with numbers**, approach these. Their local teams can approve $20K–$50K pilots without HQ involvement.

| Company | Nigerian Entity | Contact Entry Point | Budget Authority |
|---------|----------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Nestlé | Nestlé Nigeria Plc (Lagos/Agbara) | Plant Engineering Manager | Plant GM up to $50K |
| Unilever | Unilever Nigeria (Oregun, Lagos) | Maintenance & Engineering Head | Country Operations Director |
| Coca-Cola Hellenic | Nigerian Bottling Company | Plant Manager | Regional GM |
| MTN Nigeria | MTN facilities & data centres | Facilities & Infrastructure Manager | Country CTIO |
| Safaricom | Kenya data centres / base stations | Infrastructure Head | Country CTO |
| Heineken / NBPLC | Nigerian Breweries (multiple sites) | Plant Engineering | Operations Director |

**What to bring to these meetings:** 2 case studies showing % downtime reduction + ₦ saved + customer quote.

## Tier 3 — Global Multinationals (Months 9–12+)

Only approach after **5 African pilots + $100K in revenue**. By then you have a story they cannot get from Siemens:

> *"We cut downtime by 22% across 5 factories in Nigeria — dust, brownouts, heat. Siemens can't do that at our price point."*

| Company | Why They Are Interesting | Entry Strategy |
|---------|--------------------------|----------------|
| Nestlé Switzerland | Global procurement of maintenance tech | Reference from Nestlé Nigeria |
| Siemens (as partner, not competitor) | They may want to white-label our Africa-specific edge module | Tech partnership conversation |
| Schneider Electric Africa | Already invested in African industrial IoT | Joint pilot program |
| Dangote Group HQ | Nigerian conglomerate — factories across 10+ sectors | Leverage existing Tier 1 relationship |
| GE Vernova (Africa) | Power + industrial, active across Africa | Industrial IoT partnership |

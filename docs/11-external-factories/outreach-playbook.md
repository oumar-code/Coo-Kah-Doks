# External Factory Outreach Playbook

> **Project Coo-Cah | Go-to-Market**
> **Document Version:** 1.0 | **Owner:** CEO / CRO
> **Purpose:** Step-by-step playbook for finding, approaching, and converting external factories into paying pilot customers.

---

## Core Principle

Cold email and cold calls will not work in Northern Nigeria. You need **trust before transactions**. Every successful first meeting comes through a warm introduction or a shared professional context. This playbook is built around that reality.

---

## Week 1–2: Map & Warm Introductions

### 1. MAN — Manufacturers Association of Nigeria

The fastest legitimate door-opener in the NW Nigeria industrial community.

| Chapter | Location | Action |
|---------|----------|--------|
| MAN Kano Chapter | Kano | **Priority — largest industrial hub near us. Call this week.** |
| MAN Kaduna Chapter | Kaduna | Textiles + food processing presence |
| MAN Sokoto Chapter | Sokoto | Cement + agro-processing |
| MAN Zamfara Chapter | Gusau | Closest geographically to our base |
| MAN Katsina Chapter | Katsina | Cotton belt + light manufacturing |

**How to use MAN:**

1. Call the chapter secretariat and ask to attend the next monthly meeting as a guest
2. In the meeting, introduce yourself briefly:
   > *"We're building software to reduce machine downtime by 20% for manufacturers in the North. We're looking for 3 factories to run a free 30-day sensor trial. If you know anyone losing production time to machine breakdowns, I'd appreciate an introduction."*
3. Follow up with every contact the same day via WhatsApp

---

### 2. State Investment Promotion Agencies (SIPAs)

SIPAs have direct relationships with factory owners and are actively looking for technology partners to showcase to investors.

| Agency | State | Action |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Zamfara State Investment Promotion Agency | Zamfara | Schedule a meeting with the Director |
| Sokoto State Investment Promotion Agency | Sokoto | Request factory owner introduction |
| Kaduna State Investment and Development Authority (KADIPA) | Kaduna | Strong industrial database — ask for referrals |
| Kano State Investment Promotion Agency | Kano | Largest industrial base in the North |

**Script for SIPA meeting:**
> *"We are a Nigerian tech company building predictive maintenance software for factories. We want to run free pilot programs in [State] to show that local technology can reduce manufacturing downtime. We'd like you to introduce us to 2–3 factory owners who struggle with equipment breakdowns."*

---

### 3. Equipment Suppliers — Your Fastest Warm Intro Network

The people who sell generators, industrial bearings, VFDs (variable frequency drives), and spare parts to factories **know exactly which factories have chronic breakdown problems**. They are your intelligence network and your fastest referral source.

| Supplier Type | Where to Find Them | What to Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Generator / power suppliers | Industrial markets in Kano, Kaduna, Gusau | "Which of your factory customers are always calling you for emergency repairs?" |
| Industrial bearing suppliers | Kano / Kaduna machine shops | "Who buys the most bearings — they clearly have high failure rates" |
| VFD / motor suppliers | Electrical wholesalers in Kano, Lagos | "Who bought a VFD from you in the last 3 months? Their motor was already failing." |
| Maintenance contractors | Any factory maintenance outsourcing firms | Offer a referral fee — they introduce you, you both win |

**Introduction offer to suppliers:**
> *"If you refer us to a factory that signs a pilot with us, we'll give you a ₦100,000 referral fee."*

## Week 3–4: The First Meeting

### Who to Meet

Always target the **Plant Manager** or **Production Manager** first. Not procurement. Not the MD in the first meeting.

The Plant Manager:
- Feels downtime pain directly
- Has authority to approve a free trial
- Can escalate internally for the paid pilot
- Responds to operational outcomes, not software demos

---

### First Meeting Structure (30–45 minutes)

**Do not open with a product demo.**

1. **Open with their pain** (10 min):
   > *"What is your biggest production challenge right now? Which machine breaks down most often?"*
   
   Let them talk. Take notes on machine names, downtime frequency, and cost language they use.

2. **Validate the problem** (5 min):
   > *"In our experience with similar factories, [machine type] breakdowns usually cost between ₦X and ₦Y per incident. Does that match what you see?"*

3. **Introduce the offer** (10 min):
   > *"We've built a system that detects those failures 24–72 hours before they happen. It runs on a small tablet, no internet needed, and we can install it in one day. We'd like to do a free 30-day trial on your [machine name]. If we don't find and predict at least ₦500K in downtime risk this month, you owe us nothing."*

4. **Handle objections** (10 min):

   | Objection | Response |
   |-----------|----------|
   | "We already have a maintenance schedule" | "Scheduled maintenance misses random failures. 70% of factory breakdowns happen between scheduled services. We catch those." |
   | "We don't have budget" | "The trial is free. You only pay if we prove value." |
   | "I need to talk to my MD" | "Of course. Can I join that meeting? I can explain the ROI directly." |
   | "We've tried software before and it didn't work" | "Most factory software requires AC rooms and stable internet. Ours runs offline on a ₦150K tablet. It's designed for conditions like yours." |
   | "What data do you take from us?" | "We only collect vibration and temperature signals from the sensors we install. No production data, no business data. We sign an NDA before installation." |

5. **Close to next step** (5 min):
   > *"If you're open to it, let me bring my engineer next week to look at [machine name] and tell you exactly where we'd put the sensors. No commitment, just a site assessment."*

---

### What to Bring to the First Meeting

- [ ] 1-page pitch sheet (A4, printed) — see [Pilot Offer](pilot-offer.md)
- [ ] Short video demo on phone (2–3 min) showing the dashboard alert on a tablet
- [ ] Reference to any downtime data or statistics from NW Nigeria industries
- [ ] NDA template (have it ready — many plant managers will ask)
- [ ] WhatsApp number ready to exchange — this is the primary comms channel in this market

## Converting Trial to Paid Pilot

At Day 25 of the free trial, schedule a review meeting before the trial ends.

**Review meeting agenda:**

1. Present the data: anomaly events detected, alert timeline, correlation to any actual incidents
2. Calculate the ROI: `[Downtime hours prevented] × [Customer's stated cost per downtime hour]`
3. Present the paid pilot proposal: ₦2M for 6 months, covers 1–3 machines
4. If they hesitate: ask what they need to see to say yes — and deliver that

**Target close rate:** 1 paid pilot from every 3 free trials run.

## Communication Rules for This Market

1. **WhatsApp over email** — Plant managers in NW Nigeria respond to WhatsApp, not email
2. **Hausa/English mix** — Opening greetings in Hausa build trust immediately
3. **Face-to-face first** — Do not try to close a pilot over the phone; visit the site
4. **Referral-first** — Every new contact should come from an existing contact's referral
5. **Respect hierarchy** — Always greet senior staff and allow them to lead the conversation

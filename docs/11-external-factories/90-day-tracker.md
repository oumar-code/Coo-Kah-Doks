> **Purpose:** Concrete 90-day action plan to achieve the AA traction milestone — 1 paid pilot from an external factory within 90 days.

## The AA Goal

> *"Secure 1 external, paid pilot in Nigeria or Kenya to validate the $25K/yr (≈ ₦40M) price point within 6 months."*

> — Acceler8 Africa Assessment, Short-Term Growth Milestone

Our 90-day plan accelerates this. We aim to have a **signed and active paid pilot by Day 90**, with enough data and a case study to present at any AA follow-up.

### Week-by-Week Actions

| Week | Actions | Success Metric |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Week 1** | Call MAN Kano Chapter. Contact Zamfara & Kano SIPAs. Build list of 20 target factories using supplier network | 20-factory longlist ready |

## Month 2 — Install, Monitor, Generate Data

**Goal:** Sensors installed at 1–2 sites. First data flowing. First alert delivered to customer.

### Week-by-Week Actions

| Week | Actions | Success Metric |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Week 5** | Purchase sensor hardware for 3 sites. Prepare edge devices. Test offline dashboard. | Hardware ready |
| **Week 6** | Install at Site 1. Brief plant manager and maintenance lead on alert system. | Site 1 live |
| **Week 7** | Install at Site 2. Begin monitoring both sites. Send first weekly WhatsApp report. | Site 2 live. 2 weekly reports sent |
| **Week 8** | Continue monitoring. Flag any anomalies. Document all alert events. Prepare mid-trial review. | ≥ 1 alert event documented per site |

### Month 2 Deliverables

- [ ] 2 trial sites active with live data
- [ ] 8 weekly reports delivered (4 per site)
- [ ] At least 1 alert event documented with customer-verified outcome
- [ ] Gross margin tracked for both installs (hardware cost + engineer time vs. pilot fee)

## The 3 Fastest Moves Right Now

!!! tip "Do these before anything else"
    1. **Call MAN Kano Chapter this week** — Kano is the largest industrial hub close to us. Ask for the next meeting date and request to attend as a guest. Ask for the member factory list.
    2. **Drive to 1 BUA or Dangote distributor in Sokoto** — equipment suppliers know which factories have the most chronic breakdowns. These are your best referrals.
    3. **Pick your "hero machine"** — identify one machine type (e.g., a milling motor or conveyor drive) that every factory in your target sector complains about. Make that your first sensor target and build your first demo around it.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Factory manager won't give decision without MD | High | Medium | Offer to present to MD directly. Frame as 20-min ops review, not sales pitch |
| Sensor hardware delayed | Medium | High | Source locally first (Jumia Business, Kano electronics market). Only import if local unavailable |
| Trial generates no alerts — machine is fine | Low | Medium | Pre-qualify machines with supplier referrals (they know which ones are sick) |
| Customer refuses to convert to paid pilot | Medium | High | Have 3 trials running simultaneously so 1 conversion = success |
| Cash flow tight during free trial period | Medium | Medium | Keep hardware cost under ₦150K per site. Recover from first paid conversion |

# Glossary — Coo-Cah Technologies Holdings

This glossary defines key terms, abbreviations, and concepts used across all Coo-Cah documentation. When in doubt about terminology, refer to this document as the authoritative source.

---

## A

**AfCFTA**  
African Continental Free Trade Area. A free trade agreement covering 54 African Union member states, creating a unified market of 1.4 billion people with a combined GDP of over $3 trillion. Coo-Cah's pan-African sales strategy is built on AfCFTA market access.

**AI Platform (Central AI Platform)**  
The group-wide artificial intelligence and machine learning platform hosted in Rwanda by Coo-Cah Rwanda OpCo. Aggregates data from all factories, trains predictive models, and delivers optimisation decisions to MES, EMS, and other factory systems.

**AMR (Autonomous Mobile Robot)**  
A robot that navigates factory floors independently using LIDAR and cameras without fixed floor tracks. Used for intra-factory material transport, WIP movement, and waste collection. Distinguishable from AGVs (Automated Guided Vehicles) by their ability to navigate dynamically.

**ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit)**  
A semiconductor chip designed for a specific application rather than general-purpose use. Coo-Cah's Project Baobab aims to design ASICs for its own electronics products.

**ATMP (Assembly, Test, Measurement & Packaging)**  
The back-end semiconductor manufacturing process that takes a fabricated wafer, cuts it into individual dies, packages each die into a chip housing, and tests the final packaged chip. The final step before silicon becomes a usable component.

**Availability (OEE component)**  
The proportion of planned production time during which a machine or line is actually operating. `Availability = (Planned Run Time - Downtime) / Planned Run Time`. See also: *OEE*.

---

## B

**BESS (Battery Energy Storage System)**  
A bank of battery cells (Coo-Cah standard: LFP chemistry) paired with a BMS and inverter/charger to store electrical energy. Used to store excess solar generation and provide power during grid outages or night hours.

**BMS (Battery Management System)**  
The electronic system that monitors and manages a battery pack — measuring cell voltage, temperature, state of charge (SOC), and state of health (SOH), and implementing protection against overcharge, over-discharge, and thermal runaway.

**BOM (Bill of Materials)**  
The structured list of all components, sub-assemblies, raw materials, and quantities required to manufacture one unit of a product. The MES uses the BOM to issue materials and track consumption.

**Brownfield Factory**  
A factory built on or converted from an existing industrial site. Contrast with *Greenfield Factory*.

---

## C

**CapEx (Capital Expenditure)**  
Money spent on acquiring or improving fixed, long-term assets — buildings, equipment, machinery, energy systems, and technology infrastructure. Coo-Cah's phased CapEx plan is described in [Finance](./10-finance/index.md).

**CIT (Companies Income Tax)**  
Corporate tax on profits. Rate: 30% in Nigeria; 30% in Kenya; 30% in Rwanda (0–15% during investment incentive period for qualifying investors).

**CMMS (Computerised Maintenance Management System)**  
Software that manages maintenance work orders, preventive maintenance schedules, spare parts inventory, and maintenance history. Integrates with MES and AI platform for predictive maintenance.

**Cobot (Collaborative Robot)**  
A robot designed to work safely alongside humans without traditional safety caging. Force-limited and vision-guided. Used in Coo-Cah Phase 2 for assembly, inspection, and packaging tasks.

**CoGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**  
The direct costs attributable to the production of the goods sold — raw materials, direct labour, energy, and manufacturing overheads. Coo-Cah tracks CoGS per factory and per product line.

**Cognitive Autonomous Factory**  
Coo-Cah's term for Phase 3 — a factory that operates as a self-optimising system with the Central AI Platform making the majority of operational decisions.

---

## D

**Data Lake**  
A centralised repository that stores all raw and processed data from all factories. Coo-Cah's data lake is hosted in Rwanda and organised into Raw, Clean, Curated, and Serving zones.

**DFI (Development Finance Institution)**  
A financial institution that provides long-term financing and technical assistance to support economic development in emerging markets. Key DFI partners for Coo-Cah include IFC, AfDB, Proparco, and BII.

**Digital Twin**  
A virtual replica of a physical asset, production line, or factory, synchronised with real-world data in real time. Used for predictive maintenance, process optimisation, and training simulation.

**DoD (Depth of Discharge)**  
The percentage of a battery's total capacity that has been used. For LFP batteries, Coo-Cah targets maximum 80% DoD to extend battery life.

**DRC (Design Rule Check)**  
A semiconductor physical design verification step that checks whether the chip layout complies with the foundry's fabrication rules. Part of the Project Baobab ASIC design flow.

---

## E

**EDA (Electronic Design Automation)**  
Software tools used to design, simulate, and verify semiconductor circuits. Key EDA vendors include Cadence, Synopsys, and Siemens EDA. Used by Project Baobab for ASIC design.

**EIA (Environmental Impact Assessment)**  
A study required before constructing a new factory or major expansion that evaluates potential environmental impacts and proposed mitigation measures. Required by NESREA (Nigeria), REMA (Rwanda), and NEMA (Kenya).

**EMS (Energy Management System)**  
Software that monitors and controls all energy assets at a factory — solar PV, BESS, grid connection, and generators. Integrates with the Central AI Platform for group-level energy optimisation.

**EPZ (Export Processing Zone)**  
A designated industrial area offering tax and customs incentives to manufacturers that export a qualifying percentage of their output. Coo-Cah uses Kenya's EPZ for the Nairobi assembly operations.

**ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**  
Business management software that integrates all core business functions — finance, procurement, sales, inventory, HR, and production planning. Integrates bidirectionally with the MES.

---

## F

**Feature Store**  
A centralised repository of pre-computed ML features used to train and serve machine learning models consistently. Coo-Cah uses Feast as its feature store within the AI platform.

**FPY (First-Pass Yield)**  
The percentage of units that pass quality inspection on the first attempt without rework or scrap. A key quality KPI. `FPY = Good units / Total units produced`.

**FMS (Fleet Management System)**  
Software that manages the AMR fleet — task assignment, routing, charging scheduling, and status tracking. Integrates with MES for production-driven task dispatch.

---

## G

**Greenfield Factory**  
A new factory built on previously undeveloped land, without constraints from prior industrial use. Most Coo-Cah Phase 1 factories are greenfield.

**GRI (Global Reporting Initiative)**  
An international organisation providing sustainability reporting standards. Coo-Cah Holdings reports against GRI Standards for annual ESG reporting.

---

## H

**HAWT (Horizontal Axis Wind Turbine)**  
The conventional style of wind turbine with blades rotating around a horizontal axis. Coo-Cah's preferred turbine type for all wind installations.

**Holdings (Coo-Cah Technologies Holdings Ltd)**  
The apex holding company owning 100% of all Coo-Cah operating companies. Registered in Rwanda (primary) and Singapore (secondary for international investment). Owns all group IP.

---

## I

**IEC 62443**  
International standard for industrial cybersecurity — specifying security requirements for industrial automation and control systems. All Coo-Cah factories must comply.

**IFC (International Finance Corporation)**  
A member of the World Bank Group that provides investment and advisory services to stimulate private sector development in developing countries. A target DFI partner for Coo-Cah.

**IFC Performance Standards (PS1–PS8)**  
A framework of eight environmental and social performance standards used by IFC and many other DFIs as a condition of financing. Coo-Cah commits to full PS1–PS8 compliance.

**IoT (Internet of Things)**  
The network of physical devices (sensors, actuators, meters) embedded in factory equipment that collect and transmit data. Coo-Cah requires ≥ 85% IoT coverage of all factory assets by end of Phase 1.

**IP (Intellectual Property)**  
Patents, trademarks, copyrights, software licences, and trade secrets. All Coo-Cah IP is owned by *Coo-Cah IP Holdings Ltd* and licensed to OpCos under the Technology Licence Agreement.

---

## K

**Kanban**  
A lean manufacturing principle for inventory replenishment — materials are only replenished when a consumption signal (kanban card, empty bin, or electronic trigger) is received. Coo-Cah uses electronic kanban signals from MES to trigger AMR replenishment routes.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**  
A measurable metric used to evaluate performance against a target. Coo-Cah tracks OEE, FPY, OTIF, energy %, and many other KPIs across all factory layers.

**kWp (Kilowatt-peak)**  
The rated output of a solar PV system under Standard Test Conditions (1,000 W/m² irradiance, 25°C cell temperature). Used to describe the installed capacity of solar arrays.

---

## L

**LCOE (Levelised Cost of Energy)**  
The average cost per unit of energy ($/kWh) over the lifetime of an energy system, accounting for CapEx, OpEx, and total energy produced. Solar PV LCOE in West Africa is $0.04–0.07/kWh.

**LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate)**  
A chemistry for lithium-ion batteries notable for superior thermal stability, long cycle life (3,000–6,000 cycles), and lower energy density compared to NMC. Coo-Cah's mandatory BESS chemistry for safety reasons.

**Lights-Out Production**  
A production line or factory that operates without human presence on the floor. All operations are handled by robots, cobots, and automated systems. Target for Coo-Cah Phase 2 certified lines.

**LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging)**  
A sensing technology used in AMRs that emits laser pulses and measures reflections to create a 3D map of the environment for navigation. The primary navigation sensor in Coo-Cah's AMR fleet.

**LTIR (Lost Time Incident Rate)**  
A safety KPI measuring the number of workplace incidents resulting in lost work time per 200,000 employee hours. Coo-Cah target: zero LTI in any Phase Gate review window.

---

## M

**MES (Manufacturing Execution System)**  
Software that manages and monitors real-time production operations on the factory floor. The selected group standard is *Siemens Opcenter* (see ADR-002).

**MPCB (Moulded Case Circuit Breaker)**  
Electrical protection device in factory power distribution systems. Not an acronym unique to Coo-Cah — standard electrical engineering term.

**MTBF (Mean Time Between Failure)**  
Average time a piece of equipment operates between failures. A key maintenance KPI. Phase 1 target: ≥ 400 hours.

**MTTR (Mean Time to Repair)**  
Average time taken to restore a failed piece of equipment to operational status. Phase 1 target: ≤ 4 hours.

---

## N

**NAFDAC (National Agency for Food & Drug Administration and Control)**  
Nigeria's regulatory agency for food, drugs, cosmetics, chemicals, packaged water, and medical devices. Products must be NAFDAC-registered before sale in Nigeria.

**NRE (Non-Recurring Engineering)**  
One-time engineering costs incurred during the design phase of a custom semiconductor, paid to the foundry and EDA tool vendors. Part of Project Baobab ASIC tapeout costs.

**NPK**  
Nitrogen-Phosphorus-Potassium — the three primary macronutrients in fertilizers. Represents the composition of compound fertilizers produced by Coo-Cah's planned fertilizer factory.

**NPU (Neural Processing Unit)**  
A dedicated hardware accelerator for neural network inference operations. Targeted for inclusion in Coo-Cah's CCC-SOC-01 (Project Baobab Phase 2) for edge AI applications.

---

## O

**OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)**  
A composite manufacturing KPI: `OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality`. World-class manufacturing targets OEE ≥ 85%. Coo-Cah Phase 3 target is ≥ 88%.

**OpCo (Operating Company)**  
Any of the four subsidiary operating companies owned by Coo-Cah Technologies Holdings: Nigeria OpCo, Rwanda OpCo, Kenya OpCo, and Pan-African Sales.

**OPC-UA (OPC Unified Architecture)**  
An industrial communication protocol standard that enables secure, reliable data exchange between PLCs, SCADA, MES, and other factory systems. Coo-Cah's primary protocol for machine-to-MES communication.

**OTIF (On-Time-In-Full)**  
A supply chain KPI measuring what percentage of customer orders are delivered on time and in the full quantity requested. Phase 1 target: ≥ 85%.

---

## P

**Performance (OEE component)**  
The speed at which a machine operates as a percentage of its designed speed. `Performance = (Ideal Cycle Time × Total Parts) / Run Time`.

**Phase Gate**  
A formal decision point at which a factory's progress is reviewed against defined criteria before authorising advancement to the next automation phase. Phase Gate approval requires Holdings Board + Group CTO sign-off.

**PLC (Programmable Logic Controller)**  
Industrial computer used to control manufacturing equipment and processes. PLCs communicate with the MES via OPC-UA.

**PMIC (Power Management Integrated Circuit)**  
A semiconductor chip that manages power supply, battery charging, and voltage regulation in electronic devices. Coo-Cah's first Project Baobab ASIC target design (CCC-PMIC-01).

**PPM (Parts Per Million)**  
A quality metric measuring defect frequency. 1,000 PPM = 0.1% defect rate. Coo-Cah's Phase 2 supplier quality target: < 2,000 PPM.

**PPA (Power Purchase Agreement)**  
A long-term contract between an electricity generator and a buyer, fixing the price and volume of electricity to be purchased. Coo-Cah Holdings negotiates group PPAs with renewable energy developers.

**PSH (Peak Sun Hours)**  
The number of hours per day during which solar irradiance averages 1,000 W/m². Used in solar array sizing calculations. Nigeria averages 4.5–6 PSH; Rwanda averages 4.5–5.5 PSH; Kenya averages 5–6 PSH.

---

## Q

**QMS (Quality Management System)**  
The set of processes, procedures, and records that govern how product quality is planned, controlled, inspected, and improved. Coo-Cah's group QMS is certified to ISO 9001:2015.

**Quality (OEE component)**  
The proportion of produced units that meet quality standards without rework. `Quality = Good Parts / Total Parts`.

---

## R

**RDB (Rwanda Development Board)**  
Rwanda's one-stop investment promotion and facilitation agency. Coo-Cah Rwanda OpCo is registered with the RDB and benefits from RDB investment facilitation services.

**RISC-V**  
An open-source instruction set architecture (ISA) for processors. Adopted by Project Baobab as the CPU architecture for all Coo-Cah custom silicon due to its open licensing model and growing ecosystem.

**ROIC (Return on Invested Capital)**  
A profitability metric: `ROIC = NOPAT / Invested Capital`. Coo-Cah Phase 3 group target: ≥ 25%.

**RUL (Remaining Useful Life)**  
A predictive maintenance metric — the estimated remaining operational life of an asset before failure or end-of-life. Predicted by Coo-Cah's AI platform for all monitored factory assets.

---

## S

**SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)**  
An industrial control system that monitors and controls plant equipment at the process level. Provides sensor data upward to the MES and AI platform.

**SEZ (Special Economic Zone)**  
A geographically defined zone with special regulatory and tax incentives to attract investment. Coo-Cah uses the Kigali SEZ (Rwanda) and Ogun-Guangdong Free Trade Zone (Nigeria).

**SLAM (Simultaneous Localisation and Mapping)**  
The process by which AMRs build and continuously update a map of their environment while tracking their own position. Enables AMRs to navigate without fixed infrastructure.

**SOC (State of Charge)**  
The current charge level of a battery as a percentage of its total capacity. Managed by the BMS and monitored by the EMS.

**SOH (State of Health)**  
A measure of a battery's current capacity relative to its original rated capacity, degrading over time and charge cycles. Monitored continuously by the BMS.

**SON (Standards Organisation of Nigeria)**  
The Nigerian government agency responsible for setting, maintaining, and enforcing industrial standards. All Coo-Cah products sold in Nigeria must meet NIS (Nigerian Industrial Standards).

**SPC (Statistical Process Control)**  
A method of using statistical methods to monitor and control a manufacturing process. Control charts detect when a process goes out of control, allowing early intervention before defects occur.

**SoC (System-on-Chip)**  
A single integrated circuit that integrates all components of a computer or electronic system — CPU, GPU/NPU, memory controller, I/O interfaces, and more. Project Baobab Phase 2 target: CCC-SOC-01.

---

## T

**TLA (Technology Licence Agreement)**  
The inter-company agreement under which Coo-Cah Holdings licenses all group technology IP (software, AI models, MES configurations, manufacturing know-how) to each OpCo at 3% of net revenue.

**TOPS (Tera Operations Per Second)**  
A measure of AI processing performance for neural network inference. Coo-Cah's target edge AI chip: 32 TOPS NPU for factory vision inspection.

**Transfer Pricing**  
The pricing of inter-company transactions between related entities (e.g., Holdings to OpCos). Must comply with OECD arm's-length principle to avoid tax avoidance allegations.

**TRIR (Total Recordable Incident Rate)**  
A safety KPI: the number of recordable workplace injuries and illnesses per 200,000 hours worked. Coo-Cah Phase 1 target: < 1.0 TRIR.

**TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company)**  
The world's largest dedicated semiconductor foundry. Primary target fabrication partner for Project Baobab ASIC tapeouts.

---

## U

**UVM (Universal Verification Methodology)**  
A standardised methodology for verifying hardware designs using SystemVerilog. Used by Project Baobab verification engineers to validate chip designs before tapeout.

---

## V

**Vertical Integration**  
A business strategy where a company controls multiple stages of its supply chain — from raw materials to finished goods. Coo-Cah's Tier 1 factories (plastics, metallurgical) represent vertical integration feeding all other factory verticals.

---

## W

**WIP (Work in Progress)**  
Materials and components that have entered the manufacturing process but have not yet been completed into finished goods. AMRs manage WIP movement between workstations in Phase 2 and Phase 3.

**WMS (Warehouse Management System)**  
Software that manages the operations of a warehouse — inventory tracking, picking, putaway, and dispatch. Integrates with MES for BOM consumption and with the AMR fleet for goods-to-person picking.

---

## Z

**Zero Defect Manufacturing**  
A quality management philosophy aiming to eliminate all defects rather than achieve an acceptable defect level. Coo-Cah's Phase 3 quality target (< 0.3% defect rate) approaches this goal through AI-driven quality management.

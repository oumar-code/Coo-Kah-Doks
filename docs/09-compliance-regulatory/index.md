# Compliance & Regulatory Framework

## Overview

Coo-Cah operates across three distinct regulatory jurisdictions — Nigeria, Rwanda, and Kenya — each with its own legal frameworks for manufacturing, environment, standards, labour, taxation, and data protection. This document maps the key regulatory obligations for each country and identifies the responsible entity for compliance.

> **Principle:** Regulatory compliance is a minimum baseline. Coo-Cah targets to exceed local requirements and align with international standards (ISO, IFC Performance Standards, UN SDGs) as a matter of policy.

---

## Nigeria — Regulatory Framework

Nigeria is the primary manufacturing jurisdiction and therefore has the most complex regulatory environment for Coo-Cah operations.

### Company & Investment Regulation

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)** | Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020 | Company incorporation, annual returns, beneficial ownership |
| **Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission (NIPC)** | NIPC Act | FDI registration, Pioneer Status application |
| **Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)** | Companies Income Tax Act | 30% CIT; Transfer Pricing regulations |
| **Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC)** | Export (Prohibition) Order | Export documentation for eligible products |

### Manufacturing Standards

| Body | Standard/Law | Applicable Factories |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)** | Industrial Standards Act | All manufactured products |
| **SON** | MANCAP (Mandatory Conformity Assessment) | Electronics, consumer goods |
| **SON** | NIS certification | All products for domestic sale |
| **National Agency for Food & Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC)** | NAFDAC Act | Food, beverages, cosmetics, personal care, chemicals |
| **NAFDAC** | Product registration | All regulated products before commercial sale |
| **Pharmaceutical Society of Nigeria** | PSN Act | Pharmaceutical-grade chemicals |

### Environmental Regulation

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **National Environmental Standards and Regulations Enforcement Agency (NESREA)** | Environmental Standards Act | EIA for all factories, effluent discharge standards, solid waste management |
| **Federal Ministry of Environment** | National Policy on Environment | Environmental Management Plan |
| **State EPAs** (Lagos, Ogun, Delta) | State environmental laws | State-level EIA, operating permits |
| **Department of Petroleum Resources (DPR)** | Petroleum Act | Diesel storage, chemical plant environmental compliance |

### Energy & Electricity

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC)** | NESI Act | Embedded generation licence > 1MW |
| **Nigerian Electricity Management Services Agency (NEMSA)** | Electrical Installations Regulations | Electrical installation certification |
| **Rural Electrification Agency (REA)** | Electricity Act 2023 | Off-grid and mini-grid permits |

### Labour & Employment

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Federal Ministry of Labour** | Labour Act, Factories Act | Workplace safety, working hours, minimum wage |
| **Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund (NSITF)** | NSITF Act | Employer contributions for workplace injury insurance |
| **National Pension Commission (PenCom)** | Pension Reform Act 2014 | Mandatory pension contributions (10% employer + 8% employee) |
| **Industrial Training Fund (ITF)** | ITF Act | 1% of annual payroll for employee training levy |

### Data Protection

| **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** | Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 | Data processor/controller registration, data privacy compliance |
| **National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA)** | NITDA Act | IT systems notification |

## Rwanda — Regulatory Framework

Rwanda offers the most streamlined business registration in Africa. The Rwanda Development Board (RDB) operates as a one-stop shop for most permits and registrations.

### Company & Investment Regulation

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Rwanda Development Board (RDB)** | Investment Code | Company registration, FDI registration, investment certificates |
| **Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA)** | Tax Procedures Law | Corporate income tax (30%), VAT, PAYE |
| **Capital Market Authority (CMA)** | Capital Markets Law | Securities regulation for group holding entity |
| **National Bank of Rwanda (BNR)** | FX regulations | Foreign exchange transactions and repatriation |

### Manufacturing & Standards

| Body | Standard | Requirement |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Rwanda Standards Board (RSB)** | Rwanda standards (RS series) | Product certification for all goods sold in Rwanda |
| **RSB** | Certification marks | "RS" mark on certified products |
| **RDB** | Special Economic Zone (SEZ) rules | Kigali SEZ operations and incentives |

### Environmental Regulation

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Rwanda Environment Management Authority (REMA)** | Environment Law | Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) |
| **REMA** | Effluent discharge standards | Wastewater treatment compliance |
| **RDB** | Kigali Innovation City Green Building Standards | Construction and operations standards |

### Energy & Utilities

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Rwanda Utilities Regulatory Authority (RURA)** | Electricity Regulation | Licence for generation and distribution |
| **Rwanda Energy Group (REG) / Energy Utility Corporation (EUCL)** | Grid connection | Net metering and grid-tied solar agreements |

### Labour & Employment

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Ministry of Public Service & Labour** | Labour Code | Employment contracts, working hours, termination |
| **Rwanda Social Security Board (RSSB)** | Social Security Law | Pension (5% employee + 3% employer) + medical insurance (7.5% total) |
| **Workforce Development Authority (WDA)** | TVET Law | Technical vocational training partnerships |

### Data Protection

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA)** | Cybersecurity Law 2024 | Cybersecurity compliance, incident reporting |
| **Rwanda Data Protection Authority** | Law on Personal Data Protection 2021 | Data processing registration, privacy rights |

### Rwanda Investment Incentives

Coo-Cah Rwanda entities are eligible for significant investment incentives:
- **Corporate Income Tax holiday:** 0% for 7 years (investment > $50M); then 15% thereafter
- **Import duty exemptions:** Capital equipment and raw materials for qualifying investors
- **VAT exemptions:** Capital equipment imports
- **Dividend withholding tax:** Reduced from 15% to 5% for qualifying investors
- **Work permit facilitation:** Expedited permits for key foreign technical staff

## Kenya — Regulatory Framework

Kenya is Coo-Cah's logistics and East Africa distribution hub, with assembly operations through the EPZ (Export Processing Zones) framework.

### Company & Investment Regulation

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Business Registration Service (BRS)** | Companies Act 2015 | Company registration, annual returns |
| **Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)** | Income Tax Act, VAT Act | 30% CIT; Transfer pricing documentation |
| **Kenya Investment Authority (KenInvest)** | Investment Promotion Act | Investment certificate, facilitation services |

### Manufacturing & Standards

| Body | Standard | Requirement |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS)** | Standards Act | Product standards compliance, import inspection |
| **KEBS** | Diamond Mark of Quality | Local products; PVoC (Pre-Export Verification of Conformity) for imports |
| **Export Processing Zones Authority (EPZA)** | EPZ Act | EPZ licence, operating rules, benefit management |

### Environmental Regulation

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **National Environment Management Authority (NEMA)** | Environment Management & Coordination Act | EIA for all projects, audit compliance |
| **NEMA** | Waste Management Regulations | Industrial waste disposal requirements |
| **County Governments** | County Environment Laws | Local operating permits, noise, drainage |

### Energy

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Energy & Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA)** | Energy Act 2019 | Licence for embedded generation > 3kW, net metering |
| **Kenya Power & Lighting Company (KPLC)** | Grid connection | Net metering interconnection agreement |

### Labour & Employment

| Body | Regulation | Requirement |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Ministry of Labour & Social Protection** | Employment Act 2007, OSHA 2007 | Employment contracts, OSH compliance |
| **National Social Security Fund (NSSF)** | NSSF Act 2013 | Pension contributions |
| **National Health Insurance Fund (NHIF)** | NHIF Act | Health insurance contributions |
| **Directorate of Immigration** | Kenya Citizenship & Immigration Act | Work permits for foreign employees |

### EPZ Benefits (Kenya)
EPZ-registered factories benefit from:
- **10-year tax holiday** on income tax and withholding taxes
- **25% tax rate** for subsequent 10 years (vs. standard 30%)
- **Exemption from VAT and customs duty** on inputs and capital equipment
- **100% investment deduction** on capital expenditure
- **No stamp duty** on EPZ zone instruments
